Good to know

A few quick answers.

How do I install the Android app?

Download the APK on your Android device and open it. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager. Install over the previous NaviGlass release to retain app data. An up-to-date Android System WebView and WebGL support are required.

Can I download it for iPhone?

Not yet. The Android APK cannot run on iPhone or iPad. The iOS version is planned next; this page will link to a supported Apple download route when one is available.

Do I need a server or an account?

No. Local music works without an account. Navidrome is optional and requires your own reachable HTTPS server. Internet radio needs a connection, but no login.

Does it change my original music files?

No. Library access is read-only: NaviGlass does not delete tracks, change tags or upload music. Favourites and app-created playlists are saved on your device.

What should I know about this release?

This is an early, development-signed release, not a production-certified app. Full desktop feature parity, offline remote downloads, server playlist editing, equalizer and Android Auto browsing are not available. Remote title-prefix search filters the first 200 server candidates. Please share reproducible issues with your device and Android version, without passwords or private account details.
